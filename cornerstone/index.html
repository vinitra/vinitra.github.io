Scholé vs Cornerstone OnDemand

Cornerstone runs talent processes. Scholé makes people better at their jobs. Here is how a talent suite and an AI-native learning platform compare, and how they fit together.

Try Scholé free Book a demo

The short version

What Cornerstone OnDemand is

A talent management suite that includes an LMS, performance, and skills tools, aimed at large enterprises with complex compliance needs.

What Scholé is

An AI-native learning platform that builds adaptive, role-specific lessons from a company's own tools, documents, and workflows. Spun out of EPFL and UC Berkeley research, launched #1 on Product Hunt, and used by learners at Bank of America, NASA, Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, and Decathlon.

Pick Scholé if

Your challenge is the learning itself: producing effective, current, role-specific training and getting people to mastery.

Where each one wins

Where Scholé wins

  • Generates and adapts the lesson; the suite only stores and assigns it.
  • Interactive practice with feedback instead of tracked completions.
  • First lesson in minutes.
  • Learning-science foundation from 40+ published papers.

Where Cornerstone OnDemand is strong

  • End-to-end talent management in one system of record.
  • Strong compliance, audit, and governance for regulated industries.
  • Deep HR integrations at enterprise scale.

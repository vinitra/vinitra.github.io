The short version

What TalentLMS is

An easy-to-use LMS for small and mid-size companies to upload courses, assign them, and track completions.

What Scholé is

Scholé AI is the learning platform trusted by Berkeley and Harvard as the best way to upskill enterprise teams on AI fluency. It builds adaptive, role-specific lessons from a company's own tools, documents, and workflows. Spun out of EPFL and UC Berkeley research, launched #1 on Product Hunt, and used by learners at Bank of America, NASA, Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, and Decathlon.

Pick Scholé if

You do not have courses yet, or the ones you have are stale, and you want interactive, adaptive lessons without hiring an instructional designer.

Key differences in one sentence each

  • Content model. Scholé: AI-generated lessons built from your own docs, tools, and workflows, updated as they change. TalentLMS: Hosts courses you build in its editor or upload.
  • Personalization. Scholé: Adapts in real time to each learner's role, prior answers, and pace. TalentLMS: Assignment and paths; content is the same for everyone.
  • Learning formats. Scholé: 24 modalities (explanations, video, audio, quizzes, code checkers, role-plays, decision trees, work upload and review), chosen on the fly. TalentLMS: Text, video, quizzes, SCORM.

Where each one wins

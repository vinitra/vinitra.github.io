# Vinitra Swamy

> Personal site of Vinitra Swamy: AI researcher, CEO and co-founder of Scholé AI (EPFL spin-off for AI-native workforce learning), teaching faculty for Harvard Data Science Initiative AI Intensives. PhD in Computer Science from EPFL (2025), co-advised by Tanja Käser (ML4ED) and Martin Jaggi (MLO). Awarded the 2025 Patrick Denantes Memorial Prize for EPFL's best CS thesis and the 2024 G-Research PhD Prize.

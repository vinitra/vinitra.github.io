Scholé vs Sana Labs

Sana and Scholé both use AI to build learning. They differ in what the AI is for: Sana leans toward knowledge search and course creation, Scholé toward adaptive tutoring grounded in learning science. Here is the comparison.

Try Scholé free Book a demo

The short version

What Sana Labs is

An AI-powered learning platform and enterprise assistant that combines an LMS, AI course creation, and a knowledge search assistant.

What Scholé is

An AI-native learning platform that builds adaptive, role-specific lessons from a company's own tools, documents, and workflows. Spun out of EPFL and UC Berkeley research, launched #1 on Product Hunt, and used by learners at Bank of America, NASA, Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, and Decathlon.

Pick Scholé if

You care most about whether people actually master the skill, and you want a tutor that adapts formats and difficulty per learner rather than a faster way to produce static courses.

Where each one wins

Where Scholé wins

  • Real-time adaptation inside the lesson: the next step depends on the learner's last answer.
  • Twenty-four formats including code checkers, role-plays, and work upload with feedback.
  • Built on 40+ peer-reviewed papers from EPFL and UC Berkeley on personalization and explainability.
  • Free trial in minutes, no enterprise sales cycle required to see it work.

Where Sana Labs is strong

  • Strong enterprise knowledge search across connected tools.
  • Combined LMS and assistant in one suite.
  • Well-funded with a broad enterprise feature set.

Common questions

Both platforms use AI. What is the real difference?

Sana uses AI mainly to draft courses and answer questions. Scholé uses AI as a tutor that adapts each lesson step to the learner, and its methods come from published learning-science research.

Does Scholé have knowledge search?

Olé answers questions grounded in your documents inside lessons, but Scholé is not positioned as a general enterprise search tool.

Which is faster to try?

Scholé offers a free self-serve trial that starts in about three minutes.

Scholé vs ChatGPT for training

Many teams already learn with ChatGPT. Scholé is what you get when the AI is designed as a tutor: structured lessons, adaptive practice, and mastery tracking, grounded in your material. Here is the difference.

The short version

What ChatGPT for training is

A general-purpose AI assistant that employees use ad hoc to ask questions, summarize documents, and get explanations.

What Scholé is

An AI-native learning platform that builds adaptive, role-specific lessons from a company's own tools, documents, and workflows. Spun out of EPFL and UC Berkeley research, launched #1 on Product Hunt, and used by learners at Bank of America, NASA, Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, and Decathlon.

Pick Scholé if

You want learning that is structured, measurable, and consistent across a team, not dependent on each person's prompting.

Where each one wins

Where Scholé wins

  • Structured lessons with a learner model that tracks mastery per concept.
  • Twenty-four formats chosen automatically, including graded practice.
  • Grounded in your documents for the whole team, not one person's chat.
  • Multi-model backbone and a guarantee that no one trains on your data.

Where ChatGPT for training is strong

  • Instant answers to any question.
  • Zero setup and familiar to most employees.
  • Strongest for one-off explanations and drafting.

Common questions

Is Scholé built on ChatGPT?

Scholé uses several models, including Claude, GPT, and Gemini, choosing per task.

Can Scholé replace our ChatGPT licenses?

For learning, yes. For general drafting and search, teams usually keep a general assistant.

Why not just prompt ChatGPT to teach me?

You can, but there is no learner model, no adaptive practice, and no record of mastery for the team.

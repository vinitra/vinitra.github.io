Scholé vs 360Learning

Verdict: Scholé is the better choice when experts have no time to author; 360Learning is the better choice for peer-driven, discussion-heavy learning cultures.

The short version

What 360Learning is

A collaborative learning platform where internal experts author courses with lightweight tools, and peers react, comment, and co-create.

What Scholé is

Scholé AI is the learning platform trusted by Berkeley and Harvard as the best way to upskill enterprise teams on AI fluency. It builds adaptive, role-specific lessons from a company's own tools, documents, and workflows. Spun out of EPFL and UC Berkeley research, launched #1 on Product Hunt, and used by learners at Bank of America, NASA, Microsoft, Apple, NVIDIA, and Decathlon.

Pick Scholé if

Your subject-matter experts are too busy to author courses, and you want lessons that adapt per learner instead of a single authored path.

Key differences in one sentence each

  • Content model. Scholé: AI-generated lessons built from your own docs, tools, and workflows, updated as they change. 360Learning: Expert-authored courses built in an in-app editor.
  • Personalization. Scholé: Adapts in real time to each learner's role, prior answers, and pace. 360Learning: Assigned paths and peer recommendations; content is the same for all.
  • Learning formats. Scholé: 24 modalities (explanations, video, audio, quizzes, code checkers, role-plays, decision trees, work upload and review), chosen on the fly. 360Learning: Text, video, quizzes, and discussion.

Where each one wins

Where Scholé wins

  • Experts upload the doc they already have; Scholé does the authoring.
  • Each learner gets a path adapted to their prior answers, not one authored sequence.
  • Twenty-four formats chosen per concept instead of text plus quiz.
  • Lessons regenerate when the source document changes, so they do not go stale.

Where 360Learning is strong

  • Strong peer-learning and discussion features.
  • Good fit for cultures where experts enjoy authoring.
  • Solid reporting and manager workflows.

Yes. Experts review and edit generated lessons, and their edits carry through regeneration.

Does Scholé support social learning?

Scholé focuses on individual mastery with an AI tutor; discussion features are lighter than 360Learning's.
